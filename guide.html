What is FarmBox Brain+

FarmBox Brain+ is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that lets any team member record a voice note in the field, auto-transcribe it with AI, generate a summary, attach photos or videos, and send everything directly to the right Notion database — no typing required.

How to Use — Step by Step
1

Select your tab

Tap the right database tab at the top: Ugly200 (general notes), SoP (procedures), Ideas, Daily (standup), or FB1Log (greenhouse build log).

2

Set your author name (first time only)

Tap the teal Enter Author button in the header → type your name → tap OK. It's saved permanently until you change it.

3

Give your note a title (optional)

Type a short title in the Title field. If left empty, the AI summary or transcript will be used as the title automatically.

4

Record your voice note

Tap the big teal microphone button to start recording. Tap again to stop. The audio is automatically transcribed using Groq Whisper AI — you'll see the text appear in the Voice Transcription box.

5

Generate an AI summary (optional)

In the AI Summary section, tap Generate. Toggle Bullets on for bullet points. The summary also becomes the page title if you left the Title field empty.

6

Attach media (optional)

Use the Photo or Video round buttons in the Media card to attach field photos or videos. They upload directly to the Notion page.

7

Fill in tab-specific fields

Each tab has its own fields — select tags, status, phase etc. Tap + Add to create a new custom tag on the fly.

8

Send to Notion

Tap the big Send to [Tab] button. A new page is created in the corresponding Notion database with all your content, media, tags and metadata.

💡 Tip: You can also type or paste text manually in any box — you don't have to record audio. All fields are fully editable before sending.

Video AI Summary

Use this section to summarise any video content (YouTube, recordings, meetings):

1

Attach photo or video

Use the Photo / Video round buttons in the Media card above the Video AI Summary section.

2

Paste the transcript

Copy the transcript from YouTube (… → Show transcript) or any other source and paste it into the transcript box.

3

Generate summary

Tap Generate — AI returns bullet points by default. Toggle Bullets off for a paragraph. The summary is included in the Notion page when you send.
